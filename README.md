# Distributed Genius and the Generative Ratchet

*How Inherited Possibility Becomes Individual Capability, and When It Only Appears To.*

Genius is largely distributed across the exemplars, tools, and possibility-spaces a learner inherits, and education is the process that converts that inheritance into individual capability. The cumulative-culture ratchet explains how a civilization preserves its gains, not how a preserved gain becomes a person's usable skill. The paper's operational core is a distinction among three frontiers: production (what a learner can output with the tools at hand), competence (what they can independently generate and revise), and judgment (what they can reliably evaluate). Generative AI lifts production immediately while leaving competence and judgment behind; that gap is false ratcheting, and it is measurable, revealed when the tool is removed. An illustrative simulation instantiates the definitions, and the paper states its predictions as falsifiable claims with the empirical programme that would test them.
